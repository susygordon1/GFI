Our Story

9-Line Financial (9-LF) was founded by three lifelong friends who served together in the Marine Corps. That background shapes everything about how this company runs — discipline, loyalty, and showing up for each other no matter what.

What started as brothers looking out for one another turned into a mission: helping families build the strong, lasting plans they deserve — for today, and for generations to come.
