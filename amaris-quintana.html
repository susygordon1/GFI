Her Story

Amaris got her start with 9-LF after her friend Cheech set up a meeting with Tony — and it took off from there. Helping people has always been part of who she is; she was raised to help without expecting anything in return, and she's found a way to do that even in seasons when she had nothing herself.

Before financial services, Amaris built a career as a professional boxer — winning multiple national titles as an amateur, then claiming both the NABF title and an international WBC title as a pro.

What She Focuses On

Amaris is still new to the company and learning the ins and outs, but she's already clear on her purpose: helping families in any way she can. She doesn't feel called to one specific group — she's here for anyone, no limits.

Personal Touch

Amaris is a single mother to a 6-month-old baby girl, and mom to two baby goats she bottle-feeds after their mother rejected them just a week before her daughter was born. Outside of work, she stays busy working out, hiking, shooting, horseback riding, and spending time on the farm with her horses, dogs, chickens, and roosters — and of course, with her daughter.

People sometimes read her as tough at first glance — but get to know her, and you'll find a genuinely nice person who loves meeting new people.

"Learn to sit back and observe, not everything needs a reaction!"
