Fixed Index Annuity

Turn your life savings into a pension "like" income.

What it is

Think of an FIA (Fixed Index Annuity) like a protected retirement bucket: your money has the opportunity to grow with the market, but the market can't take away what you've already earned.

Who it's for

Clients approaching or in retirement who want predictable income they can't outlive.

Why FIA

Often times comes with bonuses, continual growth even when not contributing, and comes with a death benefit.

How?

Lump sum, gold buried in the back yard, 401K, IRA and other investment vehicles.

Talking point

Turn a nest egg into a paycheck that shows up for the rest of your life.
