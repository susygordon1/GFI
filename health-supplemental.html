Coverage Category

Health & Supplemental Coverage

Critical illness, hospital indemnity, and disability coverage that fills the gaps major medical leaves behind.

What it is

Supplemental health products pay cash benefits directly to the client for things major medical insurance doesn't fully cover — a cancer diagnosis, a hospital stay, or an injury that keeps someone out of work.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a financial cushion against the out-of-pocket costs and lost income that come with a health event.

Critical illness

Lump-sum cash benefit on diagnosis of a covered condition — placeholder for our specific carrier list.

Disability income

Replaces a portion of income if a client can't work due to injury or illness.

Talking point

"Health insurance covers the hospital bill. This covers everything else."
