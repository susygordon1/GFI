Coverage Category

Juvenile Indexed Universal Life (IUL)

Permanent coverage started early, so tax-free growth has decades to work in their favor.

What it is

A Juvenile IUL is a permanent life insurance policy purchased on a child's life. It locks in low-cost, lifelong coverage while they're young and healthy, and builds cash value with tax-free growth they can access later — for college, a first home, or a head start on retirement.

Who it's for

Parents or grandparents who want to give a child (ages 0–17) a financial head start — and lock in insurability before age or health make coverage more expensive.

How it works

Premiums build cash value inside the policy, growing tax-free and tied to a market index, while the child is covered for life.

Where it can help later

The policy's cash value can be accessed as an adult for college, a first home, or supplemental retirement income.

Talking point

The best time to start this is the day it's cheapest — while they're young and healthy.
