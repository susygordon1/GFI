Her Story

Destiny got into this line of work through a close friend, who introduced her to the team. Having felt the weight of financial stress herself, she's driven by a simple idea: if she can help people understand their finances and get to a better place, that's a win worth chasing — for them, and for her own family too.

What She Focuses On

Destiny enjoys helping families who are just starting out — she knows firsthand what it's like not to know where to begin. She feels especially called to serve immigrant families and young adults who may be navigating financial planning for the first time.

Personal Touch

Destiny has been with her girlfriend for 4 years — no pets yet! Outside of work, she loves painting, drawing, puzzles, and movies.

Meet her at a BBQ, and you'll find a creative, easygoing person who's just as happy working on a puzzle as she is settling in for a good movie.

"I want to help as many people as possible."
