Coverage Category

Final Expense Insurance

Simplified, affordable coverage to handle funeral and end-of-life costs.

What it is

Final expense (sometimes called burial insurance) is a smaller, simplified whole life policy designed to cover funeral costs and final wishes so loved ones aren't left with the bill.

Who it's for

Often seniors, or anyone who wants a smaller policy that's easy to qualify for.

Underwriting

Usually simplified issue — a few health questions instead of a medical exam — placeholder for our specific carrier rules.

Coverage amount

Smaller face amounts sized to funeral and end-of-life costs rather than income replacement.

Talking point

Make sure your family is remembering you, not figuring out how to pay for the funeral.
