Business Owner Strategies

Protecting the business itself — and the people who depend on it — when the unexpected happens.

What it is

Business owner strategies use life insurance to protect a company, not just a household — making sure the business can keep running, ownership stays where it belongs, and key people are covered if something happens to them.

Buy-Sell Agreements

Makes sure a business partner's share goes where it should — not to an outside party — if a co-owner passes away.

Key Person Insurance

Covers the financial hit to a business if a critical employee or owner is suddenly gone.

Business Succession Planning

A plan for who takes over — and how it's funded — when an owner retires, becomes disabled, or passes away.

Talking point

"If something happened to you tomorrow, would the business survive it?"
